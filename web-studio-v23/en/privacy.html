WHAT IS ACTUALLY SAVED

No hidden
sending.

The editor saves only explicitly saved drafts. PawMaster retains your profile and completed commands. The brief stays in sessionStorage until you close the tab or clear it.

A saved draft can be overwritten or removed in the browser's storage settings. Progress and briefs can be cleared directly in the demo. The browser creates JSON and ZIP files. We use no analytics tracking or fictional cloud synchronization.

Before actual inquiry collection is enabled, the operator must confirm their identity, public contacts, legal texts, providers and retention rules. This demo creates neither a contract nor a payment.

Temporary language-switch snapshot

When you switch the interface language, a temporary snapshot is stored in this browser to preserve your draft, undo history and entered brief. It is removed after successful restoration. If the switch is interrupted, you can clear it through the browser site-data settings. Nothing is sent to a server.
